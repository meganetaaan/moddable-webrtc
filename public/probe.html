ESP32-S3 WebRTC browser probe

Browser-side AppRTC signaling probe for Stack-chan/CoreS3 WebRTC boundary checks. It joins the signaling room, opens /ws, and exchanges browser-offerer or browser-answerer SDP plus raw ICE candidate lines.

Query parameters: ?signal=http://192.168.7.135:18091&room=stackchan&role=offerer&icePolicy=all&media=audio

Smartphone relay check through a Cloudflare Tunnel: https://stackchan.example.trycloudflare.com/probe?signal=https://stackchan.example.trycloudflare.com&room=stackchan&role=answerer&icePolicy=relay&media=audio

Configure TURN on the signaling server with TURN_URLS=turn:turn.example.com:3478?transport=udp,turns:turn.example.com:5349?transport=tcp, plus either TURN_USERNAME and TURN_CREDENTIAL, or coturn REST-style TURN_SECRET. The probe log shows relay policy and redacted ICE server schemes/hosts only. If a phone logs icecandidateerror ... code=701 ... host lookup, retry with IP-literal turn: URLs for that test run; avoid IP-literal turns: because TLS certificate names will not match.

The signaling room replays only one fresh connected offerer's offer to an answerer. After an answer consumes that offer, later answerers trigger a reoffer-request so the CoreS3 can close the stale peer and publish a new offer.
